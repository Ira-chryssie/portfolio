About Me

Passion-driven engineer building real solutions with AI and embedded systems.

I'm an Embedded Systems student at the University of Burundi, specializing in Applied AI & Cybersecurity. I design and deploy production-grade fullstack web applications with Django, and build end-to-end machine learning pipelines across tabular, image, and audio data using scikit-learn and PyTorch.

In 2026, I was selected among Burundi's national representatives to two international olympiads — the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI) in Astana, Kazakhstan, and the International Cybersecurity Olympiad in AI (ICOA) in Australia — earning Honourable Mentions in both.

I also won the National Award in Burundi's Cybersecurity & AI Competition by developing a technical solution evaluated by a national jury, ranking among the top entrants nationwide.

When I'm not coding, I'm contributing to the AI community through Deep Learning Indaba and IndabaX Burundi, and exploring the frontiers of embedded AI.
